## Art Direction Rationale (v0.6.1)

**Decision:** the visual standard moves from a *cold, desaturated neutral* palette to the *warm wood/earth + cream + coral* palette of `wathersoup.jpeg`, applied as a **1:1 mockup-fidelity target**.

**Why:**
1. **Reference authority.** `wathersoup.jpeg` is the single approved visual reference. Specs that contradict it (turquoise found cells, `#A0BFC7` header plates, `#EFEFEE` background) can never reach parity — they are structurally different colour families, not tuning deltas.
2. **State legibility.** The coral found state (`#FD6D52`) on cream cells (`#F1EDE1`, 16.3:1 surface separation) distinguishes idle vs found by both fill *and* border (`WS-EncontradoBorde` `#8E3B33`, 6.4:1), which the previous turquoise-on-pale reading achieved less clearly against a light board.
3. **Palette coherence with the celebration.** The warm confetti ramp (`#FD6D52`, `#B45252`, `#EDE19E`, `#FDC109`) belongs to the same family as the board, removing the leftover cold accents (`Turquesa`, `Cian`) that read as foreign on a wood surface.
4. **Scoped, reversible governance.** `WS-*` is an **additive, Word-Search-local** extension — it renames no global token and mirrors the precedent set by `CreamYellow` (v0.5.1) and `CelebrationRed` (v0.5.0). The global matrix stays untouched; other minigames are unaffected.
5. **Accessibility preserved.** All `WS-*` pairings were recomputed (WCAG): body text ≥ 4.5:1 (`TextDark` on cream 16.5:1, on wood 4.9:1, on coral 6.8:1), actionable borders ≥ 3:1 (`WS-MaderaBorde` 3.56:1), and the title is explicitly required to carry a `WS-MaderaSombra` outline because bare `#65B9C4` on wood is only 1.7:1.

**Supersedes:** the v0.4.x–v0.6.0 "strict cold-neutral restriction" (`Placa`/`HeaderBannerColor`/`SubheaderBannerColor`/`Turquesa` only) — valid only within Word Search. The V1 file freeze referenced in v0.5.x–v0.6.0 is released; V1 `.tscn` may be deleted once Phase 12 lands and parity is re-verified.

**Phase ordering (recommended):** proceed in numeric order; Phases 5–7 are independent cleanup (no design input needed). Phases 8–9 (dual-mode clock) require design input before starting — hold at Phase 7 until clock semantics are agreed. Phase 11 is unblocked and may be done after Phase 7.
